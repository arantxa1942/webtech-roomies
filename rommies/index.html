Find a room or list one

Roomies is a platform where people who have a free room in a shared house or apartment can offer it to people looking for a place to live. The host publishes the room with the necessary details for seekers to apply, visit, and move in.

Find a room Publish a room

How it works

  • Publish

    A host when register a property, describes the apartment or house in whitch the room is, also what the room itself has, the financial details and finally the household rules

  • Apply

    The seeker can apply to a published listing. The application must contain a message introducing themselves, the date they would like to move in, and how long they intend to stay. The application follows a lifecycle which could be: pending, shortlisted, accepted, rejected, and withdrawn. A seeker may not apply twice to the same listing.

  • Visit and Move In

    Once the application is shortlisted, the host arranges a visit to the property on a given date and time. After the visit takes place, it is marked as completed. When the application is accepted, the others are instantly rejected and the listing moves to reserved.
